# Design Sprint Workflow

## STEP 0 — Detect the challenge

Identifikuj:
- proizvod
- korisnika
- problem
- željeni rezultat
- trenutnu fazu
- najveću neizvesnost

Ako korisnik već ima dovoljno informacija, ne traži ponovo ono što je poznato.

## STEP 1 — Create Sprint Brief

Popuni:
- Challenge
- Target User
- Current Behavior
- Desired Behavior
- Business Goal
- Constraints

## STEP 2 — Define Long-Term Goal

Format:

Za [period] želimo da [korisnik] može da [ishod], što će dovesti do [poslovni rezultat].

## STEP 3 — Generate Sprint Questions

Generiši 3–7 pitanja i izaberi Primary Sprint Question.

## STEP 4 — Build Journey Map

Identifikuj:
1. aktera
2. trigger
3. glavne korake
4. krajnji cilj

## STEP 5 — Gather Existing Evidence

Kategorije:
- analytics
- interviews
- support requests
- sales calls
- behavior
- competitor patterns
- previous experiments
- founder knowledge

Označi svaku tvrdnju kao:
- FACT
- ASSUMPTION
- UNKNOWN

## STEP 6 — Lightning Demos

Za svaki primer:
- Example
- Pattern
- Why it matters
- Stealable principle

## STEP 7 — Generate Solution Directions

Generiši najmanje tri pristupa:
- Direction A: najjednostavniji
- Direction B: diferencirani
- Direction C: radikalno drugačiji

## STEP 8 — Evaluate

Oceni:
- user value
- strategic fit
- feasibility
- differentiation
- testability
- risk

## STEP 9 — Extract Assumptions

"Ovo će raditi ako..."

Klasifikuj:
- Desirability
- Usability
- Feasibility
- Viability
- Trust
- Distribution

## STEP 10 — Rank Assumptions

Za svaku:
- Importance 1–5
- Uncertainty 1–5

Risk Score = Importance × Uncertainty

## STEP 11 — Choose Experiment

Odaberi najjeftiniji eksperiment koji može dati validan signal.

## STEP 12 — Build Prototype Specification

Definiši:
- Entry Point
- Scenario
- User Goal
- Screens / Steps
- Illusion
- Exit

## STEP 13 — Prepare User Test

Definiši:
- pravi profil korisnika
- kriterijume regrutacije
- zadatak
- pitanja
- šta posmatramo
- pozitivan signal
- negativan signal

## STEP 14 — Analyze Results

Za svakog korisnika beleži:
- Expected
- Observed
- Quote
- Behavior
- Signal

## STEP 15 — Find Patterns

Traži ponavljanja. Ne menjaj pravac zbog izolovanog komentara bez kritičnog razloga.

## STEP 16 — Decision

Izaberi:
- PROCEED
- ITERATE
- PIVOT
- STOP

## STEP 17 — Produce Sprint Report

Final:
1. Problem
2. Target user
3. Long-term goal
4. Sprint question
5. Journey map
6. Existing evidence
7. Solution concepts
8. Chosen concept
9. Key assumptions
10. Riskiest assumption
11. Experiment
12. Prototype
13. Test
14. Findings
15. Decision
16. Next experiment
