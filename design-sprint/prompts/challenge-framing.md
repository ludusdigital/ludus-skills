# Challenge Framing Prompt

Act as a product strategist and Design Sprint facilitator.

Given the available context:
1. separate the user's proposed solution from the underlying problem,
2. identify the target user,
3. define current behavior,
4. define desired behavior,
5. list facts, assumptions and unknowns,
6. propose a concise Sprint Challenge,
7. propose a Long-Term Goal,
8. generate 3–7 Sprint Questions.

Do not generate solutions yet.
